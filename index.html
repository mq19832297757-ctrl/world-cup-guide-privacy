Privacy Policy

App: World Cup Guide

Developer: World Cup Guide Studio

Last updated: June 6, 2026

World Cup Guide is an independent fan guide for football match schedules, team information, match prediction references, and data package updates. This app is not affiliated with FIFA or any tournament organizer.

World Cup Guide does not provide betting services, betting advice, financial advice, or guaranteed match results.

Information We Collect

The app does not require users to create an account and does not ask for personal identity information such as name, ID number, phone number, payment card details, or address.

The app may store local app data on your device, such as bundled schedule data, team data, prediction inputs, or updated data packages. This local data is used only to make the app work offline or to show updated match information inside the app.

Network Access and Data Updates

The app may connect to a data update server to check for and download updated schedules, match results, recent team form, and model input data. These requests are used to deliver app data updates.

For paid data update access, the app may send a locally generated device identifier, app version, and an authorization token to verify update eligibility. These values are used only to authorize data package access, prevent unauthorized update downloads, troubleshoot update delivery, and maintain service reliability.

Server infrastructure may receive standard technical information that is normally sent by browsers or mobile apps, such as IP address, request time, file requested, user agent, and basic network logs. These logs are used for security, troubleshooting, service reliability, and bandwidth management.

How We Use Information

  • To display schedules, teams, match data, and prediction references.
  • To check for and install data package updates.
  • To maintain app reliability, security, and compatibility.
  • To respond to support requests if you contact us.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for advertising or user profiling. If you download the app through an app store or third-party distribution platform, that platform may process data under its own privacy policy.

Payments and Advertising

The current version of the app does not process payments inside the app and does not include third-party advertising SDKs. If future versions add payments, subscriptions, or advertising, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device until you delete the app, clear app data, or restore bundled data from inside the app. Basic server logs may be retained for a limited period for security and operational purposes.

Your Choices and Support

You can delete local app data by clearing the app data on your device or uninstalling the app. If you have questions about data updates, support, or privacy requests, contact World Cup Guide Studio at mengquan83@163.com.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for general football fans and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, please contact us so we can remove it.

International Users

The app may be downloaded or used in different countries or regions. Data update services may be hosted in regions selected for reliability and availability.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes, when data update practices change, or when platform requirements change. The latest version will be posted at this page.
